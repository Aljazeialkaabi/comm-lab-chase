THE STORY

THE CHASE

The film follows a person who is being chased by someone. The reason behind the chase is never explained, leaving the audience to wonder who is chasing them and what they are running from.

THE INSPIRATION

We were inspired by horror and thriller films, as well as more recent films such as "Backrooms." This influenced the tone, color, and overall aesthetic of our shots. We wanted the film to feel unsettling without explaining everything to the viewer.

MADE IN 30 MINUTES

We had to create the film in only 30 minutes, which meant we had to make decisions quickly and work with whatever we had around us. Because of this, the film has a very spontaneous feeling.
